**Regime grid as run. Otherwise the outcome is a tie. A diverged or failed run loses to every comparator; a comparison whose comparator diverged or failed is not decided. Observers are compared on the noise realisation the PINN was trained on (r = 0); rows marked * use more information and are never winners; two-seed and one-seed PINN rows are labelled and do not decide H1-H7.
